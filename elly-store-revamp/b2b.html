Quick answers

Before you start a quote

Which items can I order, and are adult sizes available?
You pick from the same catalog as the store — including Customization pieces. All three order types include adult sizes. As you build your quote, each item shows its own size run (adult products show XS and up), so you'll see exactly what's available for each product before you request.
What are the minimums?
Bulk / wholesale: 10 units per design. Corporate gifting: 20 units. Corporate events: 20 units. If you're close to a minimum, start the quote anyway — our team confirms feasibility with your estimate.
Can I get samples or speak to someone first?
Yes — call or WhatsApp +65 9628 1037 to request samples or speak with our sales team before you start.
How long does an order take?
Your quote request is reviewed within ~2 working days. For ready-stock items with personalisation, production takes up to 14 working days after you confirm your order. Deadlines under 3 weeks are flagged for rush review by our team — they confirm feasibility before you commit.
What artwork do I need to provide?
High-res logo or artwork — PNG, AI, SVG or PDF, 300dpi preferred; vector artwork is best for screen print and embroidery. If you only have a rough idea, that's fine — our team helps during review.
How do payment and invoicing work?
You only pay after approving your confirmed quote — by card, bank transfer or Net terms where available. An official invoice is issued with your order.
Do I need to be a registered business?
Bulk / wholesale is for retailers and resellers — we may ask for your business registration. Corporate and event orders don't require one; HR teams, founders and event organisers order directly.
Where do you ship?
Singapore and worldwide, matching the store. Shipping cost is confirmed with your quote before you pay — one bulk shipment to a single address, or split across multiple locations. Freight and duties apply where relevant.
