When will I receive it?
Production takes up to 8 weeks after the order window closes. We email progress updates at design approval and shipping.
Why full payment upfront?
Every pre-order is guaranteed production — full payment lets us commit to the run with the factory the moment the window closes. No deposits, no partial payments.
Can I cancel or change my order?
Changes are welcome before the order window closes. After production begins, exchanges follow our standard 30-day policy once you receive the item.
