Growth & Pre-Order dashboard

Shopify's Admin is a system of record — it tells you what sold. This is the decision layer on top of it: are we on pace to clear the factory MOQ, why is each design pacing that way, and which run actually earns the most. Everything below is arranged as seven questions in the order an owner would ask them. Tiles and figures are illustrative (PRD §9); demand moves with real demo checkouts.

One bar per day of the window per design, on a single shared scale: solid = orders to date, outlined = what the projection implies for the days left. This is the clearest place to watch a scenario button lengthen every bar at once.

What is not real: tile values, historical sell-through and margin figures are illustrative placeholders (PRD §14). There is no live Shopify API call and no forecasting model — pace is a trailing average, and the projection band is an illustrative spread, not a confidence interval (PRD §9). Demand is real: it is the pre-order tally your own demo checkouts write.
Decision clock & stress (timeline from the Pre-Order PDP): the window closes 25 Sep 2026, production takes up to 8 weeks after close, and payment is taken in full upfront. Full payment makes every pre-order final at close — the PDP promises changes only before the window closes and exchanges after — so the cancellation stress exists only inside the window and zeroes out on close day, and the production-slip stress moves the ship-by date instead of refunding anyone. The push cost (S$800) and lift (+10% of the projected tail) are illustrative. Every pre-order is guaranteed production, so the commit decision is how much to produce above the floor — never whether to run.
